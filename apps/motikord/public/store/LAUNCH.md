# Motikord launch posts

Launch posts for X and LinkedIn, and the 10-second launch video for each. The copy comes from `STORE.md` and the approved design frames. It never names an AI brand.

> Motikord is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. The link below is its fixed listing address and opens once the listing is published. Post after that.

## Videos
The videos are in the project files, in `motikord/launch/`. Each is 10 seconds, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC sound (the bundled CC0 track from Motikord, made in this repo).

| Platform | File | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | `motikord-launch-x-1920x1080.mp4` | 1920x1080 (16:9) |
| LinkedIn | `motikord-launch-linkedin-1080x1350.mp4` | 1080x1350 (4:5) |

Each video has a poster PNG next to it, for a thumbnail or a still post. The videos are rendered from the approved design frames; the source is in the project files, in `launch-video-source/`.

**Alt text (both platforms):** Motikord launch video. A clip description goes into the Motikord panel, then the editor opens with scenes on a timeline. Text: Describe a clip. Get a motion video. Add to Chrome. It's free.

## LinkedIn
Post with the LinkedIn video attached. Put the link in the post, not in a comment.

```text
Motikord is live in the Chrome Web Store.

Motikord turns a prompt, a page or your own media into a short animated clip.

• Describe a clip and Motikord picks the type: promo, quote, chart reveal, explainer, logo intro, captions or social post
• Keep editing with prompts like "slower" or "bigger title", or by hand in the editor, with undo
• A quality check looks at text size and reading time before you export
• Add a brand kit, your own media, and free photos, videos and music from open media libraries
• Export MP4, WebM or GIF in several sizes

The editor, preview, render and library stay on your computer. No account, no server, no upload. To write the clips, connect the AI service you choose with your own key.

It's free and open source.

Add it to Chrome: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pppjklpibpnndjfddhcedbdicfdalchl
Read the code: https://github.com/Itzfeminisce/appskord/tree/main/apps/motikord

#ChromeExtension #OpenSource #MotionGraphics
```
