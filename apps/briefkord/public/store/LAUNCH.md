# Briefkord launch posts

Launch posts for X and LinkedIn, and the 10-second launch video for each. The copy comes from `STORE.md` and the approved design frames. It never names an AI brand.

> Briefkord is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. Replace `STORE_LINK` with its listing link before posting.

## Videos
The videos are in the project files, in `briefkord/launch/`. Each is 10 seconds, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC sound (the bundled CC0 track from Motikord, made in this repo).

| Platform | File | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | `briefkord-launch-x-1920x1080.mp4` | 1920x1080 (16:9) |
| LinkedIn | `briefkord-launch-linkedin-1080x1350.mp4` | 1080x1350 (4:5) |

Each video has a poster PNG next to it, for a thumbnail or a still post. The videos are rendered from the approved design frames; the source is in the project files, in `launch-video-source/`.

**Alt text (both platforms):** Briefkord launch video. A meeting board with pages, notes and a flow diagram, then meeting mode shows one card full screen. Text: One board for every meeting. Add to Chrome. It's free.

## LinkedIn
Post with the LinkedIn video attached. Put the link in the post, not in a comment.

```text
Briefkord is live in the Chrome Web Store.

Briefkord gathers everything for a meeting on one board.

• Save the page you are on in one click, with its title, preview and text
• Drop in images, PDFs and documents, or add notes, checklists and flow diagrams
• Arrange cards on a free canvas, or see them as an agenda with time for each section
• Meeting mode shows one card at a time, full screen, with your notes beside it
• Share a board as a file anyone can open in a browser

Boards, files and settings stay on your computer. No account and no server of its own. Optional helpers, like summarizing a page or drawing a diagram from a description, use the AI service you choose with your own key.

It's free and open source.

Add it to Chrome: STORE_LINK
Read the code: https://github.com/Itzfeminisce/appskord/tree/main/apps/briefkord

#ChromeExtension #OpenSource #Meetings
```
