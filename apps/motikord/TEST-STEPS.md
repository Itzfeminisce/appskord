# Test steps (v3)

Windows laptop, Chrome.

1. Unzip the new zip over your `Motikord` folder.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`.
3. Press Reload on Motikord. Never press Remove: it wipes your clips.

## Check
1. Open the side panel. What's new shows once. Close it and reopen: it does not show again.
2. Type "a bar chart of sales by month" and press Make clip. Check the key frames, approve them, watch the motion.
3. Open the editor. Press play.
4. Change a layer, then press Undo.
5. Press the Voice button. Turn on Auto captions. Check the captions appear.
6. Open the Data tab. Paste a small table. Check the chart changes.
7. On Home, press "Start from a template". Pick one.
8. Press Export. Pick MP4 and 60 fps. Play the file.
9. Export again with 30 fps. Play the file.
10. Press Check. Read the quality check.
11. Open Settings. Check the keys row.

Tell me anything that looks wrong or feels slow.
