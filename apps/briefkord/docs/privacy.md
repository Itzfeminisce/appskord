# Briefkord privacy policy

Briefkord is a Chrome extension that gathers everything for a meeting on one board. This page says what stays on your computer, what leaves it, and when.

## What stays on your computer
Your boards, cards, notes, layout, the images and files you add, the pages you save and your settings are stored in your browser, on your computer. Briefkord has no account, no server of its own and no sync. If you remove the extension, that data goes with it.

## What leaves your computer, and when
Briefkord sends data out only to do something you asked for, and only to the service that does it.

- **Saving a page:** Briefkord reads the title, address, preview image and text of the tab you save, only when you press Save, the shortcut or the right-click menu. Nothing is sent anywhere. It reads the page again only when you press Refresh from the page.
- **AI actions:** when you press Summarize, Diagram from a description, Suggest an order, Brief me or Write this with AI, only the text that action needs (a saved page's text, your description, card titles and notes) goes to the AI service you chose (Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Gemini or xAI). Images and files you add are never sent.
- **Dictation:** while the mic is on, audio is held in memory only. When you stop, that audio goes once to the speech service you chose (Groq or OpenAI) with your own key, and is not kept. Esc while listening throws it away.
- **Your keys:** if you connect Penv Cloud (https://penv.cloud), Briefkord asks it for your AI keys so you don't paste them in. Keys are kept in session storage, cleared when Chrome closes, and only sent to the service they belong to.
- **Sharing:** a view file or an edit file is saved to the folder you choose. It goes wherever you send it. Briefkord never uploads it.

Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Briefkord does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

## What Briefkord does not do
- No tracking, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- No reading of the pages you visit. Briefkord reads a tab only when you save it.
- No host access at install. Chrome asks you once for each AI service, and for Penv Cloud, inside the click that needs it.

## Your control
Delete a card or a board and it is gone from your computer, with its files. Settings, Storage shows how much space boards use. Disconnect Penv Cloud or remove a pasted key at any time in Settings.
