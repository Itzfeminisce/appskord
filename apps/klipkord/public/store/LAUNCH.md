# Klipkord launch posts

Launch posts for X and LinkedIn, and the 10-second launch video for each. The copy comes from `STORE.md` and the approved design frames. It never names an AI brand.

## Videos
The videos are in the project files, in `klipkord/launch/`. Each is 10 seconds, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC sound (the bundled CC0 track from Motikord, made in this repo).

| Platform | File | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | `klipkord-launch-x-1920x1080.mp4` | 1920x1080 (16:9) |
| LinkedIn | `klipkord-launch-linkedin-1080x1350.mp4` | 1080x1350 (4:5) |

Each video has a poster PNG next to it, for a thumbnail or a still post. The videos are rendered from the approved design frames; the source is in the project files, in `launch-video-source/`.

**Alt text (both platforms):** Klipkord launch video. The Klipkord panel starts a screen recording, then the review screen shows the video with written notes and chapters. Text: Record your screen. Get the notes written. Add to Chrome. It's free.

## LinkedIn
Post with the LinkedIn video attached. Put the link in the post, not in a comment.

```text
Klipkord is live in the Chrome Web Store.

I wanted a screen recorder that writes the notes for me and keeps the video on my own computer. So I built one.

Klipkord records your screen and your voice, then writes it up:
• A screen, window or tab, with computer sound and an optional camera bubble
• A live transcript while you talk, and a better one after you stop
• A summary, notes and chapters shaped to the recording: a how-to guide, a bug report, a team update or a meeting
• Trim the video, jump to any moment from the transcript, and add comments

Recording, the video and the library stay on your computer. No account, no server, no upload. Transcription and notes can run entirely on your computer. If you want faster results, you can connect the AI service you choose with your own key.

It's free and open source.

Add it to Chrome: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/iapgbifdchhmidblniddncnagghloepi
Read the code: https://github.com/Itzfeminisce/appskord/tree/main/apps/klipkord

#ChromeExtension #OpenSource #Productivity
```
