## What needs written permission

- Publishing a fork, build or modified version under any of the names above, or under a confusingly similar "-kord" name.
- Using the icons or logos, or ones that look like them, on your own app or store listing.
- Anything that suggests your version is the official one, or is made or endorsed by the original author.

## If you fork

Before you publish a fork to any store or website:

1. Give it a new name that does not end in "-kord" and is not confusingly similar.
2. Replace the icons, logos and store art with your own.
3. Change the extension name and description in each app's manifest and store listing.
4. Keep the copyright and licence notices, and share your source as the AGPL-3.0 requires.
