# Rekord launch posts

Launch posts for X and LinkedIn, and the 10-second launch video for each. The copy comes from `STORE.md` and the approved design frames. It never names an AI brand.

## Videos
The videos are in the project files, in `rekord/launch/`. Each is 10 seconds, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC sound (the bundled CC0 track from Motikord, made in this repo).

| Platform | File | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | `rekord-launch-x-1920x1080.mp4` | 1920x1080 (16:9) |
| LinkedIn | `rekord-launch-linkedin-1080x1350.mp4` | 1080x1350 (4:5) |

Each video has a poster PNG next to it, for a thumbnail or a still post. The videos are rendered from the approved design frames; the source is in the project files, in `launch-video-source/`.

**Alt text (both platforms):** Rekord launch video. A live meeting transcript fills in, then turns into a summary with action items and decisions. Text: Your meetings, written down for you. Add to Chrome. It's free.

## LinkedIn
Post with the LinkedIn video attached. Put the link in the post, not in a comment.

```text
Rekord is live in the Chrome Web Store.

Rekord writes down your meetings for you.

• Record a meeting in a Chrome tab or in a desktop meeting app, with your microphone if you choose
• A live transcript shows while people talk
• Get a summary and action items when it ends
• Ask questions about one meeting, or across all of them
• Rekord remembers promises, decisions and questions, each with the quote it came from
• Open loops shows what you owe and what you're owed, with a brief before each meeting

Meetings, transcripts and settings stay in your browser. No account and no server of its own. It works with the AI service you choose, using your own key, or with a model running on your own computer.

It's free and open source.

Add it to Chrome: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/flkicdahoghegnlgpkmdcemlooblblcd
Read the code: https://github.com/Itzfeminisce/appskord/tree/main/apps/rekord

#ChromeExtension #OpenSource #Meetings
```
